Our Story

Since 1960

The Classic Restaurant began as the famous shop of Totaram Sindhi Mithaiwala — a name synonymous with quality, tradition, and an unusual love for the delicious. For over six decades, we have been serving the people of Sitapur with authentic flavours, handcrafted in pure desi ghee.

"An Unusual of Delicious — where every bite carries the taste of tradition."

From our restaurant serving over 100 dishes to our mithai counter overflowing with handmade sweets, bakery cakes and premium bites — The Classic is Sitapur's most beloved food destination.
